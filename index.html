Intersectionality · Identity · Neurodiversity

Reimagining spaces that weren't designed for us.

My work lives at the intersection of felt experience and educational policy. I am drawn to the unwritten curricula and erased histories—specifically the lived experiences of queer communities, communities of color, and the neurodiverse.

"I’m not here to simply study identity—I’m here to live through it, challenge it, and create space for those who’ve never been invited into the conversation."
