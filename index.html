Exploring the felt experience of identity within systems.

I am a researcher focused on how identity is lived and contested in spaces not designed for intersectional realities. My work spans the adjustment of Latiné students at HSIs, the implementation of queer health education, and the evolving role of AI in community companionship.

Selected Work

Queer Sex Ed & Policy Implementation

Current Analysis

AI & Queer Companionship

Current Study

Investigating how queer communities utilize generative AI as a surrogate for emotional companionship and support.

Identity Construction in Adolescents of Color

Ongoing Research

Qualitative work on how queer adolescents of color navigate and articulate their identities.
